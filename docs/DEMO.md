# Crucible: a four-minute saved-evidence demo

Use the existing local runtime and built app with `npm start`, then open [Crucible](http://127.0.0.1:4201). Every step below reads retained evidence. No downloads, provider calls, preparation, fitting or replay are needed.

## 1. Start with a completed argument

Choose **Explore completed experiments** beside the wheat introduction. The [recorded-conclusions view](http://127.0.0.1:4201/?view=history&outcome=recorded) shows four folios with conclusions; **All outcomes** also preserves two unfinished QA/recovery folios. The three Occupancy records include replays of the same data, not three independent confirmations.

Open [A controlled nonlinear sensor system](http://127.0.0.1:4201/?folio=synthetic&stage=folio). Its retained result is **+44.94 percentage points** in mean group-level balanced accuracy across 24 generated groups. Read the scope directly below: this is an authored XOR generator, conditional on its fixed training set and seeds. It demonstrates a supported conclusion within that synthetic setting.

Use **Inspect the evidence** to examine the saved computation checks. The stage navigation also lets you read the contract, discovery observations and registered confirmation without running them again.

## 2. Show the negative result and its limits

Open [Office occupancy under sensor loss](http://127.0.0.1:4201/?folio=occupancy&stage=folio). The retained candidate-minus-baseline effect is **−1.57 percentage points** across six usable calendar days. The verdict stays **inconclusive** because independence of adjacent days is unestablished. The population is one office room, and the result does not establish equivalence or generalization to other buildings.

This is a useful contrast with the synthetic case: the workbench preserves the measured direction and the uncertainty instead of turning every completed workflow into a success claim.

## 3. Return to the photograph

Open [the wheat study](http://127.0.0.1:4201/). Its saved status is **Prepared corpus · Detector not trained · No measured conditions**. The corpus contains 3,653 audited photographs; all eight detector conditions remain unrun, with zero local final exposures. A completed wheat experiment and final wheat export are not available.

Toggle **Source annotations**, then open **Box records** and select an annotated target. The 45 released boxes on this discovery photograph are source labels. They are not detector predictions, matched detections, misses or measured errors. Selecting another condition only changes which saved condition is inspected.

## 4. Open the provenance

Scroll to **Open the notebook** to inspect the retained source fields, protocol, training and custody records. Follow the source links only if you want to inspect the cited material. The UI distinguishes prepared source material from detector execution and final evaluation.

Keep the demonstration to navigation, filters, layers, box selection and evidence inspection. **New folio**, source compilation/retrieval, preparation, training, execution, registration and evaluation are outside this saved-evidence walkthrough.

The scientific limits and retained-state details are documented in [wheat validation](wheat-validation.md), [corpus qualification](wheat-corpus-qualification.json), and [legacy methods](scientific-methods.md).
